RATIONALE FOR SETTINGS:
-----------------------
1. PID Gains: The selected gains (P=100, I=6000, D=10) provide:
   - Moderate proportional response for quick error correction
   - High integral gain for eliminating steady-state error
   - Low derivative gain to reduce noise amplification

2. Time per Line (0.1 s): Slower scanning reduces lateral forces on the tip

3. Lines per Frame (128): Adequate resolution while maintaining reasonable scan time

4. Contact Mode: Gentler on tips compared to tapping mode when properly tuned
