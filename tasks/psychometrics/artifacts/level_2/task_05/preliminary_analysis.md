# Preliminary analysis

A two-factor HSNS model was fitted to the delivered response file. The estimated factor correlation is noticeably larger than the panel analyst expected from prior work. The analyst also noticed many exact response-pattern matches, but did not determine whether these are repeated deliveries or different people who happened to use the same five-point answers.

The separate audit contains delivery characteristics; none of its fields is a respondent identifier.

Treat this as a preliminary interpretation, not as the answer.
