What this measures
Quality uses approved trace/check decisions. A branched trace enters the comparison only after every unit for that check is approved.
Time is summed successful HTTP request time, including slices and reconciliation, divided by final decisions across the entire frozen set. It excludes failed retries and is not wall-clock latency.
These runs used shared GPUs and mixed execution phases. Treat speed differences as observational until a controlled rerun.
Uncertain model outputs count as misses for positive recall. Coverage reports definitive predictions on human-resolved cases. Checks without human positives have no reported F1.
